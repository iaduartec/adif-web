# Inglés A2 — 10 preguntas originales

**Instrucciones:** Elige una sola opción en cada pregunta.

1. My brother ___ to work by bus every day.
   - A) go
   - B) goes
   - C) is go
   - D) going

2. We ___ dinner when the phone rang.
   - A) have
   - B) had
   - C) were having
   - D) are having

3. There isn't ___ milk in the fridge. Could you buy some?
   - A) many
   - B) a few
   - C) much
   - D) several

4. The station is ___ the post office and the library.
   - A) between
   - B) across
   - C) during
   - D) behind of

5. “I’m exhausted.” — “You ___ go to bed earlier tonight.”
   - A) should
   - B) mustn’t
   - C) can’t
   - D) were

6. Choose the correct sentence.
   - A) She has visited Rome last year.
   - B) She visited Rome last year.
   - C) She visit Rome last year.
   - D) She was visit Rome last year.

7. At a café: “Would you like anything to drink?” — “___, please. Just water.”
   - A) No, thanks
   - B) No, I don’t
   - C) Not drink
   - D) I wouldn’t

8. Read the text and answer the question.

   *On Saturday, Leo took the 9:15 train to Greenford. He planned to visit the science museum, but it was closed for repairs. Instead, he had lunch in a small café near the station and walked beside the river. He returned home at six o’clock.*

   Why didn’t Leo visit the science museum?
   - A) He missed the train.
   - B) The museum was closed.
   - C) He had no time before lunch.
   - D) The river was too far away.

9. Read the text in question 8. Where did Leo have lunch?
   - A) On the train
   - B) At the museum
   - C) In a café near the station
   - D) At home

   - A) The meeting room was yesterday.
   - B) You can’t finding it here.
